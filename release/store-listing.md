# NMMS Spark — store listing draft

App name: NMMS Spark

Short description: Practice NMMS questions in Tamil and English, with offline tests and explanations.

Full description:

Build a daily NMMS practice habit with NMMS Spark. Practise Mental Ability, Mathematics, Science and Social Science in Tamil and English.

• 25,000 bundled practice questions available offline from the first launch.
• Timed practice sets with explanations after each answer.
• Review correct, incorrect and unanswered questions.
• Retry mistakes and see your recent practice history.
• Switch between Tamil and English.
• Download additional questions from a configured teacher question feed when online.

Student registration and teacher progress sharing are present in the current build. Final wording for those features depends on the release privacy decisions.

NMMS Spark is an independent practice app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official application of an examination authority. Practice questions do not guarantee an exam result.
